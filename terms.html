6. Subscriptions & Billing

Plans

CoupleFit offers a free Basic plan and a paid Premium plan. Elite is coming later. One Premium subscription covers both partners: the partner who buys it pays, and the linked partner gets the same access for as long as the subscription is active and your accounts stay linked.

When one partner stops

The subscription belongs to the partner who bought it (the “subscriber”). Only the subscriber can cancel it, through their own App Store or Google Play account. If the subscriber cancels, both partners keep Premium until the end of the period already paid for, after which both accounts move to the free Basic plan. If the partners disconnect their accounts, the linked partner loses access to the subscriber’s Premium right away.

The other partner can start their own Premium subscription at any time with their own App Store or Google Play account. Their CoupleFit account, data and progress stay as they are; a new account is not needed. If the partners stay linked, the new subscription covers both of them.

Apple and Google do not allow a subscription to be transferred to another person, and we cannot refund or pro-rate overlapping periods if both partners subscribe at the same time. Founding couple pricing stays with the subscription it applied to and does not transfer to a new subscription started by the other partner.

Payment and renewal

Subscriptions are bought and billed through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically at the end of each period (monthly or yearly) unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

The price is shown in the app before you buy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple or Google account settings, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Deleting your CoupleFit account does not cancel your subscription.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. This does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

Founding couple pricing

The first 500 couples who sign up and confirm on our waitlist get 30% off the regular Premium price.

The founding price is linked to the CoupleFit account that uses your waitlist email address, and it applies for as long as your subscription stays active without interruption, including renewals and switching between monthly and yearly billing.

If you cancel and your subscription ends, the founding price no longer applies, and if you subscribe again later, the regular price applies. Founding pricing cannot be transferred to another account.

If you upgrade to Elite once it is available while your subscription stays active, your founding discount carries over to Elite.

Prices, currencies and rounding

Prices are set per country or region and shown in local currency by the App Store or Google Play.

Each store only allows certain price points, so our prices are rounded to the nearest available local price point. As a result, the percentages we mention, such as the founding couple discount of 30% and the saving on yearly billing compared with monthly billing, are approximate and may differ slightly by currency, country, store and plan.

Prices shown on our website in EUR, USD, GBP, CAD and AUD are for information only; in other countries the store shows its own local price. Whether taxes such as VAT, GST or sales tax are included depends on your country.

The price shown in the App Store or Google Play before you confirm your purchase is the price that applies to you. If a price on our website differs from the price in the store, for example because of an error, a currency conversion or a later price change, the store price applies.

Price changes

We may change our regular prices in the future. Apple or Google will let you know in advance and, where required, ask for your consent before a higher price applies to your subscription.

7. Invite a Couple (Referral Program)

How it works

If you have joined our waitlist or have a CoupleFit account, you get a personal invite link. You can share it with another couple (the “Invited Couple”). The same link is also used to invite your own partner; inviting your own partner is not a referral and does not earn a reward.

Reward for the Invited Couple

An Invited Couple who signs up through a valid invite link gets their first 30 days of Premium free when they start their first Premium subscription in the app after launch.

This applies only to new subscribers: neither partner may have had a CoupleFit subscription or free trial before. The free period is offered through the App Store or Google Play and is subject to their eligibility rules.

After the free period, the subscription renews automatically at the price shown in the app, unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the free period ends. If an app store does not allow this offer, we may offer an equivalent benefit in another form. If the Invited Couple also qualifies for founding couple pricing, their founding price applies after the free period.

Reward for you

You get one month of Premium free for each qualified referral. A referral qualifies when all of the following are true:

  • the Invited Couple signed up through your invite link or entered your invite code;
  • both partners of the Invited Couple created a CoupleFit account and linked their accounts;
  • the Invited Couple has had a paid Premium subscription for at least 30 days after any free period, it is still active, and no payment has been refunded or charged back;
  • the Invited Couple is a different couple: not you or your partner, not someone in your household, and neither partner had a CoupleFit account or subscription before.

Limits

You can share your link as often as you like, but you can earn a reward for up to 3 qualified referrals per couple per calendar year. Each Invited Couple can lead to only one reward, no matter which partner signed up through the link. Rewards are given per couple, not per person.

How you receive the reward

Depending on your platform and subscription status, we give the free month by extending your current subscription (moving your next renewal date), by giving you a free promotional Premium period, or through an App Store or Google Play offer.

Because of app store limits, we may combine rewards or apply them at a later renewal. Your CoupleFit account must be active when the reward is applied, and as with any Premium subscription, your linked partner gets the same access. Rewards have no cash value and cannot be exchanged, transferred or sold.

Invites before launch

Invites sent before the app launches count once the app is live, as long as the Invited Couple uses the same email address in the app as on the waitlist, or enters your invite code, and the referral qualifies within 6 months after the app launches.

Before launch, a referral does not give any reward yet. Invite rewards are separate from founding couple pricing: an invite does not give anyone a founding spot.

Our records decide

Whether a referral exists and qualifies is determined by our records, such as the invite link or code used, the waitlist email address, and how the accounts are linked.

The choice made on the sign-up form (“my partner” or “another couple”) is used as an indication only. If an invited person links their account with the person who invited them, they are treated as partners and not as a referral.

Fair use

Share your link only personally and with people who would want to hear from you. You may not:

  • use your link in paid advertising or in ads that use the CoupleFit name;

You are responsible for following the law when you share your link.

Where possible, we will tell you why and give you the chance to respond at support@couple-fit.com.

Changes to the program

We may change, pause or end the referral program at any time, for example if app store rules change. Rewards you have already earned before a change stay valid, unless they were earned through misuse. We will announce important changes on our website or in the app.
