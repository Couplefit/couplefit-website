How to Delete Your CoupleFit Account

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your account. Note: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.

Steps

  • Open the app → Settings → Legal → Delete Account
  • Confirm deletion

What happens when you delete

  • Your profile and stored data are removed from our systems
  • Your partner connection is unlinked

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel billing. Manage your subscription via your platform:

  • Apple: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Google Play: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions

Need help?

Contact support@couplefit.app if you need assistance.
