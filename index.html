Our Story

CoupleFit started with two people who believed that life becomes better when you grow together.

Why We Started

Our story started long before CoupleFit existed. We first met in the Netherlands through a course, but it wasn't until we both found ourselves in Melbourne, Australia, that our friendship turned into something more.

What brought us together wasn't just attraction. It was understanding. For the first time, we both felt truly seen, heard and understood.

Our relationship began with distance, uncertainty and the challenges that come with building a life while travelling the world. Those experiences taught us something we still believe today: the strongest relationships are built when two people choose to grow together.

How CoupleFit Was Born

Like many people, we found ourselves stuck in unhealthy routines. Long hospitality shifts, irregular schedules, stress and little structure slowly drained our energy. We knew we wanted something different.

As we focused on training together, eating better and creating healthier habits, our lives began to change.

At the same time, we noticed something surprising: every fitness app was designed for individuals. Nothing existed for couples. No place to share goals, build routines together or stay accountable as a team.

That realization became the foundation of CoupleFit.
