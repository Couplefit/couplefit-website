Features

Connect as a couple

You each create your own account, then link them with one invite code. From that moment you share a dashboard with each other's progress, goals and streaks.

Your own level, one plan

Each of you gets workouts that fit your body and goals. Train side by side or at different times, and see the moment your partner finishes their session.

Our Story

CoupleFit started with two people who believed that life becomes better when you grow together.

  1. The Netherlands

    Where we first met

    We first met in the Netherlands, through a course we were both following.

  2. Melbourne, Australia

    From friends to partners

    It wasn't until we both found ourselves in Melbourne that our friendship turned into something more. What brought us together wasn't just attraction. It was understanding: for the first time, we both felt truly seen, heard and understood.

  3. On the road

    Growing through distance

    Our relationship began with distance, uncertainty and the challenges of building a life while travelling the world. It taught us that the strongest relationships are built when two people choose to grow together.

  4. Everyday life

    Stuck in unhealthy routines

    Long hospitality shifts, irregular schedules, stress and little structure slowly drained our energy. When we started training together, eating better and building healthier habits, our lives began to change.

  5. The idea

    Nothing existed for couples

    Every fitness app was designed for individuals. There was no place to share goals, build routines together or stay accountable as a team. That realization became the foundation of CoupleFit.

  6. Today

    Building CoupleFit together

    We're building the app we wished we had, and we're inviting our first couples to help shape it.
