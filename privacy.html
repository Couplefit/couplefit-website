Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CoupleFit collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application, website, and related services.

1. Who We Are & How to Contact Us

CoupleFit is currently run by Max Peters and Rachelle Sarkis, who are responsible for your personal data (the “controller”). Our company, CoupleFit OÜ (Estonia), is being incorporated; once it is registered, it will take over this role and we will update this policy with its details.

Email: support@couple-fit.com | Website: https://couple-fit.com/

2. What Data We Collect

Account & Profile, Fitness & Wellness, Partner Connection, Support; device logs and analytics; app store subscription status; AI providers (pseudonymized).

3. How We Use Your Data

Provide and operate the app; personalize experiences; process subscriptions; communicate; improve and secure our services.

4. Legal Bases

Contract, Legitimate Interests, Consent, Legal Obligation.

5. Sharing

Processors (e.g., Firebase, analytics, RevenueCat, Brevo for our waitlist and emails, and Umami for anonymous website statistics), app stores, partner links, and where required for legal/safety reasons.

Website statistics

To understand how many people visit couple-fit.com and sign up for the waitlist, we use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service with its data stored in the European Union. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personal data that identifies you: we only see aggregated statistics, such as the number of visits, the pages viewed, the referring website, and the type of device and browser. We use this based on our legitimate interest in improving our website.

Subscriptions and payments

Payments for Premium are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or bank details. To manage subscriptions in the app, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States). RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous user ID that the app creates for your account, your purchase and subscription history (such as your plan, when it started, renews or ends, and the price and currency), the receipt data the app store provides, and basic technical information such as your app version, device type, country and IP address.

We use this to check whether you have Premium, to give your linked partner access to the same subscription, to show you the right prices, and to understand how our subscriptions perform. Your linked partner can see whether your couple has an active subscription, but never your payment details. The legal basis is the performance of our agreement with you, and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and improving our service.

If you joined our waitlist as one of our first 500 founding couples, we mark your app account as a founding couple so the app can show you founding prices. We do this by matching the email address of your app account with your confirmed waitlist sign-up. If your founding subscription ends, we remove this mark.

6. Data Retention

We retain data as needed for the purposes described. Data may be deleted or anonymized upon account deletion. Subscription and purchase records are kept for as long as you have an account, and afterwards only for as long as tax and accounting laws require (usually up to 7 years).

7. Account Deletion & Choices

In‑app: Settings → Legal → Delete Account. Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions.

8. Data Security

HTTPS/TLS, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure.

9. International Transfers

Some of our service providers process personal data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States, for example RevenueCat (subscriptions) and Google (Firebase). In those cases, we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards at support@couple-fit.com.

10. Children’s Privacy

Not intended for users under 16.

11. Your Rights

Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restriction, Portability, Objection, and Withdraw Consent where applicable. To use any of these rights, email us at support@couple-fit.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in the country where you live.

12. Third‑Party Links

We are not responsible for external sites’ policies.

13. Founding Couples Waitlist & Emails

When you join our founding couples waitlist on couple-fit.com, we collect your email address and, if you choose to provide them, your partner's first name and whether you are signing up together. We also create a personal invite code for you. If you joined through someone else's invite link, we store their code and whether you told us they are your partner or another couple, so we can link you to your partner or credit the invite under our referral program (see section 7 of our Terms of Service). Once the app is live, we use this together with your app account (your waitlist email address and how accounts are linked) to check whether an invite qualifies for a reward. We do not share your details with the person who invited you; we only let them know when an invite has qualified. We use your email address to confirm your sign-up (double opt-in), to send you a welcome email, and to keep you updated about CoupleFit's launch and early access, based on your consent. We do not ask for or store your partner's email address; your partner only hears from us if they sign up themselves. We use Brevo (Paris, France), an EU-based email service, to store this data and send these emails. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in our newsletters, or ask us to delete your waitlist data by emailing support@couple-fit.com. We keep this data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Storage in your browser

Our website saves a few small things in your browser’s local or session storage: your personal invite code and your partner’s first name (if you entered it) after you sign up, the invite code of the person who invited you if you came through their link, your chosen currency on the pricing section, and your progress on your challenge page. We do this only so these pages work for you. This information stays on your device, is not used for tracking or advertising, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. We do not use cookies on couple-fit.com.

Welcome challenge

After you confirm your sign-up, we send you a personal link to our free 4-week couples challenge on couple-fit.com. To make this work, we create a random personal code and store it with your contact details in Brevo. On the challenge page, we save what you enter so you can come back later and see your progress: the first names you fill in, the boxes you tick, your answers to the optional questions and plans, and any feedback you choose to give us about the challenge and the app. This is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in a data center in the European Union, and is linked to your personal code, not to your name or email address. We use this data only to show you and your partner your own challenge, based on our agreement to provide it to you, and we read your feedback to improve CoupleFit. Anyone who has your link can open your challenge page, so please only share it with your partner. The questions are optional; please do not enter health information or anything you would not want your partner to read. We keep this data until you unsubscribe from our emails or ask us to delete it, and then we delete it automatically.
