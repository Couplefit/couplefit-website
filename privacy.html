Last Updated: October 9, 2026

2. What Data We Collect

Account & Profile, Fitness & Wellness, Partner Connection, Support; device logs and analytics; app store subscription status; AI providers (pseudonymized).

Provide and operate the app; personalize experiences; process subscriptions; communicate; improve and secure our services.

Contract, Legitimate Interests, Consent, Legal Obligation.

Processors (e.g., Firebase, analytics, RevenueCat, and Brevo for our waitlist and emails), app stores, partner links, and where required for legal/safety reasons.

We retain data as needed for the purposes described. Data may be deleted or anonymized upon account deletion; certain receipts may be retained where required by law.

7. Account Deletion & Choices

In‑app: Settings → Legal → Delete Account. Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions.

8. Data Security

HTTPS/TLS, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure.

9. International Transfers

Standard contractual safeguards may apply where required.

10. Children’s Privacy

Not intended for users under 16.

11. Your Rights

Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restriction, Portability, Objection, and Withdraw Consent where applicable.

12. Third‑Party Links

We are not responsible for external sites’ policies.

13. Founding Couples Waitlist & Emails

When you join our founding couples waitlist on couple-fit.com, we collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your partner's email address. We use your email address to confirm your sign-up (double opt-in), to send you a welcome email, and to keep you updated about CoupleFit's launch and early access, based on your consent. Your partner's email address is only used to link your accounts once the app launches, based on our legitimate interest; we do not send marketing emails to your partner unless they sign up themselves. We use Brevo (Paris, France), an EU-based email service, to store this data and send these emails. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in our newsletters, or ask us to delete your waitlist data by emailing support@couplefit.app. We keep this data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

14. Changes

We may update this Policy; continued use means acceptance.
