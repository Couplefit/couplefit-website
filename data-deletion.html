Data Deletion Request

If you want to request deletion of specific personal data associated with your CoupleFit account (without deleting your entire account), or remove your details from our founding couples waitlist, follow the guidelines below.

What you can request to delete

  • Workout history
  • Nutrition or meal data
  • Body metrics (weight, height, measurements)
  • Photos you uploaded
  • Partner sync data
  • General activity logs
  • Waitlist data (your email address and, if provided, your partner's email address)

How to submit your request

Send an email to support@couplefit.app with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and include:

  1. The email address associated with your CoupleFit account or waitlist sign-up
  2. Which data you want removed
  3. Any relevant details to help us identify the data

How data deletion works

  • We delete the requested data from our servers
  • We remove associated entries (logs, sync records, meal data, analytics events) where applicable
  • For waitlist data, we remove your details from our email list provider (Brevo)
  • We confirm via email once the deletion has been completed

Important notes

  • Deleting specific data will not delete your full account
  • You may continue using CoupleFit normally after data deletion
  • Subscription billing (App Store/Play Store) is not affected
  • Only want to stop receiving our emails? Use the unsubscribe link in any of our newsletters
