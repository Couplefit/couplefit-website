Billing and cancellations

  • Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of your current period to avoid renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Deleting your CoupleFit account does not cancel your subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. CoupleFit cannot issue refunds itself.

Prices, currencies and rounding

Prices are set per country by the App Store and Google Play and rounded to local price points. Because of this rounding, the founding discount can be slightly more than 30% in some currencies, and the yearly saving can also differ slightly. The price shown in the store before you buy is the price you pay. See section 6 of our Terms of Service.
