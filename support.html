CoupleFit Support

Need help? Here are answers to the most common questions. Can’t find what you need? Email us at support@couple-fit.com and we’ll get back to you within 2 working days.

Getting started

When can I download CoupleFit?

CoupleFit launches in early summer 2027 on iPhone and Android. Join the founding couples waitlist and we’ll let you know the moment it’s live.

How do I link my account with my partner?

Each of you creates your own account. One of you shares your invite code from the app, and the other enters it. Your accounts are then linked, and you can see each other’s progress.

Can we use CoupleFit if we don’t live together?

Yes. You each train on your own schedule and in your own place, and you still see when the other one shows up.

Subscriptions & payments

Does one subscription cover both of us?

Yes. One Premium subscription covers both linked accounts. See our plans for details.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CoupleFit
  • Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CoupleFit

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel your subscription.

I paid, but I don’t have Premium

Make sure you’re signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you paid with, then tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen in the app. Still not working? Email us with the date of purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, see Google Play refunds.

How does founding couple pricing work?

The first 500 couples on our waitlist get 30% off Premium for life, as long as they stay subscribed. Because store prices are rounded, the discount can be slightly more than 30% in some currencies. Sign up for the app with the same email address you used for the waitlist, and you’ll see your founding price automatically. If you cancel and subscribe again later, the regular price applies.

Your account & data

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Legal → Delete Account. No access to the app? See how to delete your account.

How do you use my data?

Read our Privacy Policy. You can also request deletion of specific data.

How do I leave the waitlist?

Click the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or email us with the subject “Delete my waitlist data”.
